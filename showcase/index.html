          What exactly does the readability check inspect?
          

            At least 20 words; at least 45% of word tokens have three or more
            characters; at least 55% of visible characters are alphabetic; no
            replacement or null characters; and at least one clinical keyword.
            Formatting is normalized in a copied report. Clinical wording is
            preserved. A failed report receives no report loss, while its
            patient still participates in outcome learning.
